🎓 Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) Market Mechanics

Master essential market infrastructure concepts: Primary vs. Secondary markets, T+2 Settlement timelines, and Central Depository Company (CDC) account custody.

Primary Market (IPO) vs. Secondary Market (PSX Trading Floor)
Primary Market (IPO)

Direct Company Capital Raising

  • Issuer: Company sells newly issued shares directly to investors.
  • Price: Fixed IPO price or determined via Dutch Auction Book Building.
  • Capital Flow: Money goes directly to the company for business expansion.
  • Example: Initial Public Offering (IPO) of Air Link, Octopus Digital.
⚡
Secondary Market (PSX)

Investor-to-Investor Trading Floor

  • Traders: Investors buy and sell existing shares among each other.
  • Price: Real-time supply & demand matching via KATS (Karachi Automated Trading System).
  • Capital Flow: Money transfers between buyer and seller (company gets ₨0).
  • Example: Daily trading of UNITY, OGDC, TRG on PSX screen.
The PSX T+2 Settlement Cycle Timeline

Trades executed on PSX take 2 business days to settle cash and deliver shares electronically via NCCPL & CDC.

Day T (Trade Date)

Order Execution

Buyer and seller orders match on PSX KATS engine. Contract note is generated.

➔
Day T+1 (Clearing)

NCCPL Netting & Clearing

National Clearing Company (NCCPL) calculates net cash and share obligations for all brokers.

➔
Day T+2 (Settlement)

CDC Delivery & Cash Transfer

Shares are electronically credited to buyer's CDC Sub-Account; seller receives funds.

Role of Central Depository Company (CDC)
🔒

Dematerialized Shares Custody

Replaces physical paper share certificates with electronic 100% secure depository accounts registered in your CNIC.

🆔

Investor Account System (IAS)

Direct CDC IAS account gives 100% individual control independent of broker insolvency or custody risk.

📲

eAccess & Dividend SMS Alerts

Instant SMS & email notifications from CDC whenever shares enter/leave your sub-account or cash dividends credit.
