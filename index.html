PSX Undervalued Stock Analyzer — Methodology

Dual-Engine Relative & Intrinsic Valuation Protocol
Core Valuation Principle: A stock is UNDERVALUED when its market price sits below a defensible estimate of intrinsic value, with a margin of safety wide enough to absorb estimation error. Never evaluated from relative multiples alone.

Step 1 — Relative Multiples

Computes trailing P/E (ratio of Price to EPS), P/B (Price to Book Value), Dividend Yield, EV/EBITDA, and the classical Graham Number \(\sqrt{22.5 \times \text{EPS} \times \text{BVPS}}\).

Step 2 — Relative Multi-Factor Score (0-100)

  • P/E Component (30%): Rewards P/E below sector average. P/E < 3 is treated as an earnings distress risk, not a bargain.
  • P/B Component (20%): Rewards P/B below sector average. P/B ≤ 0.3 is flagged as solvency risk.
  • Dividend Yield Component (20%): Rewards yield above sector average, capped if payout ratio > 80%.
  • EV/EBITDA Component (20%): Rewards capital-structure neutral operating multiple below sector.
  • Leverage Penalty (10%): Points subtracted if Debt-to-Equity exceeds sector peers by > 25%.

Step 3 — Absolute Intrinsic Valuation Hierarchy

  1. DDM (Gordon Growth Model): Primary for consistent dividend payers with stable/growing 5-year payouts. Dividend growth \(g\) is capped at Hurdle Rate \(- 1.5\%\).
  2. DCF (Discounted Cash Flow): 3-Year Free Cash Flow forecast discounted at Required Return (\(\text{Risk Free} + \text{ERP}\)) with terminal value.
  3. Graham Number: Used as conservative asset-and-earnings sanity floor when historical cash flow projections are unavailable.

Step 4 — Margin of Safety & Core Verdict

\(\text{Margin of Safety} = \frac{\text{Fair Value} - \text{Price}}{\text{Fair Value}} \times 100\%\)

  • 💎 Undervalued: Relative score in top tertile (≥ 66.7) AND Margin of Safety ≥ 15%.
  • 🤔 Possibly Undervalued: Meets either relative score or intrinsic margin gate.
  • ⚖️ Fairly Valued: Margin of Safety between -10% and +15%.
  • 🔴 Overvalued: Relative score in bottom tertile (≤ 33.3) AND Margin of Safety ≤ -10%.

Step 5 — Quality / Liquidity Risk Gates

Automatically downgrades any Undervalued stock to Undervalued (Caution) if Free Float < 10%, one-off non-recurring earnings are present, EPS is negative, or Debt/Equity is more than double sector average.
